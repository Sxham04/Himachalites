      Authors

      Who can sign in, and what they can do.

        Adding someone

        1. In the Supabase dashboard, open Authentication › Users › Invite user and enter their email.
        2. Once they accept, they appear in this list with no access.
        3. Set their role to Author and save. Authors can write and publish their own stories.

        Your account doesn't have access yet

        Ask the Himachalites admin to give you the Author role, then reload this page.
